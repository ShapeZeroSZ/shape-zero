LINK SECTOR SCOPING -- DERIVATION, HYPOTHESES AND PREDICTIONS (committed with the check script, before any
run), 2026-09-28. Open item: MODEL_SPEC sec 9, "the link sector in the continuum reading". Checks:
shape_zero_tests/link_scoping_checks.py.

SET-UP. Links as in model.py / MODEL_SPEC sec 3: F_n = c (W_n v_{n+1} - W_{n-1} v_{n-1}), W symmetric and
commuting with JJ, i.e. W = g rho(H) with H Hermitian on C^n (g a configuration strength, g-hat = gc/sqrt K,
MODEL_SPEC 1b). In complex form the link force is c g H (psi'_{n+1} - psi'_{n-1}). Genuine parameters:
c-hat, kappa-hat, beta-hat. Linear stiffness K = sqrt5; Q(k) = K + 2c(1 - cos k).

DERIVATION 1 -- uniform link, q = 1, per Fourier mode (exact on the lattice, linear order).
 For a plane wave chi e^{i(kn -+ w t)} with H chi = h chi, the link term is -+ 2 c g h w sin k chi, so
    a-branch (e^{-iwt}):  w^2 + (kappa + 2cgh sin k) w - Q(k) = 0
    b-branch (e^{+iwt}):  w^2 - (kappa + 2cgh sin k) w - Q(k) = 0
 The two positive roots satisfy (Vieta), EXACTLY, for every eigen-direction h, strength g, kappa, K, c:
    (S) SPLITTING RULE   w_b(k) - w_a(k) = kappa + 2 c g h sin k
    (P) PRODUCT RULE     w_a(k) w_b(k)   = Q(k)   -- the link-free, spin-free value, the same for every h.
 At fixed frequency the same relation reads w^2 +- kappa w = K + 2c - 2c sqrt(1 + (g w h)^2) cos(k -+ theta),
 tan theta = g w h: a Peierls shift theta per eigen-direction, opposite on the two branches, with tan theta
 proportional to the branch's own lab frequency and to h -- AND a hopping renormalisation
 c -> c sqrt(1 + tan^2 theta), which is not a gauge phase. (S) and (P) are the fixed-k reading of both.
 Consequences:
  - Trace part (H = I): a common Peierls shift = a Galilean drift in the continuum (sec 1c) plus the
    hopping renormalisation (the continuum's s^2 = c + V^2): removable by one frame.
  - Traceless part: component-dependent Peierls shifts and drifts V_j ~ -c g h_j w/(w + kappa/2); no single
    frame removes them. In the free linear theory each is removable by a component-dependent boost -- a
    transformation that is not a symmetry once components interact (the shared radius of A', or
    non-commuting links elsewhere).
  - For generators with unequal |eigenvalues| (e.g. lambda_8: 1, 1, -2 over sqrt3) the renormalisation is
    component-dependent -- a gauge-invariant, frame-invariant effect: tan theta_3 / tan theta_1 = -2 exactly,
    so theta_3 / theta_1 != -2 (an ordinary unitary link would give angles in the eigenvalue ratio); and
    arctan(g w lambda_8) carries a trace part (2 arctan x - arctan 2x)/3 != 0 (x = g w/sqrt3): a traceless
    link produces a colour-blind Peierls part at third order.
  - kappa enters only through w: the branch Peierls angles obey tan theta_b / tan theta_a = -w_b/w_a
    (opposite charges, magnitude in the ratio of lab frequencies; sec 1c's "kappa physical through links").

DERIVATION 2 -- q >= 2, links of non-commuting generators on different axes (e.g. g sigma_1 on x-bonds,
 g sigma_2 on y-bonds). Per mode the link matrix is 2 c g w (sin k_x sigma_1 + sin k_y sigma_2): the internal
 eigenbasis is locked to the propagation direction (axis at angle atan2(sin k_y, sin k_x)), with band
 touchings at (0,0), (pi,0), (0,pi), (pi,pi) of winding +1, -1, -1, +1 (sum 0). This is a non-Abelian field
 strength -i[A_x, A_y] != 0 from uniform links -- the genuinely non-Abelian content, absent in q = 1. The
 axis angle and the windings are independent of every parameter. model.py implements links along axis 0
 only; a test needs the same passive link term on a second axis (same coupling class, not a new coupling).
 Non-commuting segments along one line (q = 1) give order-dependent holonomies -- the existing gates 6-7.

HYPOTHESES
 H1 At linear order and fixed frequency the link sector is exactly the matrix Peierls form plus the hopping
    renormalisation, per eigen-direction; (S) and (P) hold exactly for model.py's force.
 H2 The only link consequences that are independent of the genuine parameters and not already implied by
    counted census rows are: (P) (the even part of the dispersion, unchanged in the branch product by any
    link), and, in q >= 2, the direction locking and windings of Derivation 2.
 H3 Of these, (S) is not independent: it is row 1's two-branch rule applied per eigen-direction, with row 5
    at g = 0. The windings and locking follow from row 2 (per-axis sin k, transverse independence) and the
    su(2) algebra (structural, row 10): verified consequences, not independent. (P) is the one candidate
    genuine prediction: it constrains the even part of the dispersion, which rows 1, 2, 5 and 12 do not, and
    it would fail if the link coupled other than linearly in velocity, or as an ordinary unitary link.
