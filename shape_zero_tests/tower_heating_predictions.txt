HYPOTHESIS (recorded as untested in 6s). The D <= 8 part feels the upper levels only as an extra
stiffness Delta(x,t) = |u| - |u_l|, a function of s(x,t) = |u_upper(x,t)|^2. The one-way gain is
parametric heating: the packet absorbs energy from the random, site- and time-dependent part of that
stiffness. The reproducible phase shift is a separate, refractive effect of the populated
background's mean size.

(2) TIME DEPENDENCE OVER LONG RUNS (T = 20000, incoherent, A_U = 0.005; D16 seeds 0, 1; D64 seed 0).
    No reversal: f > 0 throughout [2000, 20000]. Rough saturation estimate: if the populated D <= 8
    component (256 modes) comes to equipartition with the upper modes (~3e-5 energy per mode at
    A_U = 0.005), f saturates near +9% at any D. So:
    D16: roughly linear -- rate over [15000, 20000] / rate over [2000, 7000] in [0.6, 1.2];
         f(20000) in [+1%, +3%].
    D64: bending toward saturation -- ratio in [0.3, 1.0]; f(20000) in [+3%, +7%].

(3) NO SITE-TO-SITE VARIATION -> NO GAIN.
 3a x = 0 (the upper levels coherent and uniform: s constant in space and time at t = 0):
    |rate| < 5% of rate(x = 1).
 3b U2 (uniform in space, oscillating in time at w_a(0) + w_b(0) = 3.14): the modulation can only
    create packet pairs where 2 w_a(k) + kappa = 3.14, i.e. at k = 0, where the packet (k0 = pi/2,
    width 8) has no content: |rate| < 10% of the incoherent rate at the same <s> (A_U = 0.005).
