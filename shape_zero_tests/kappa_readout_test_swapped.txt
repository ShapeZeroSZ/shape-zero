==============================================================================
HOW TO READ IT
==============================================================================
  * plane-wave kappa must be ~-0.018 for a readout to be valid
    (pinned_asymmetry_reference.py: -0.0184 +/- 0.00033).
    a readout that breaks the plane wave is wrong; ignore its rows.
  * if 'weighted' and 'centre' are FLAT across side while 'uniform'
    falls  ->  H1 CONFIRMED, readout artifact. Fix the readout and
    re-measure kappa(w). The kappa(w) formula must not be published.
  * if all three fall together  ->  H2, the side-dependence is
    physical; the question becomes what sets the isolated-beam limit.
  * pinning |d|/|th| must be 1.000 +- 0.001 everywhere. If it breaks,
    something more basic is wrong and kappa is moot.
