Second-variation computation (v5.3) — the worked appendix
Setting. By Liero–Mielke–Savaré, HK(μ₀, μ₁) is realized as the Wasserstein distance on the cone C(Ω) between optimal lifts, and constant-speed HK geodesics are projections of cone geodesics: each elementary particle moves along a cone geodesic, which develops to a straight segment in a Euclidean plane with the radial coordinate r as distance from the origin. The distance to the null measure satisfies HK(μ, 0)² = m(μ), the total mass, so φ² = c·m with c a convention constant.
Lemma (mass is exactly quadratic along HK geodesics). Let (μ_t), t ∈ [0,1], be a constant-speed HK geodesic of length L. Then F(t) := φ(μ_t)² satisfies F″(t) = 2L² identically. Proof: along a straight segment z(t) = (1−t)z₀ + t·z₁ in the development plane, r(t)² = |z(t)|² is quadratic with second derivative 2|z₁ − z₀|² = 2·d_C(z₀, z₁)². Pure-reaction motions (creation/annihilation, radial in the cone) are the degenerate case of the same formula. Integrating over the optimal geodesic plan: m″(t) = 2∫d_C² dπ = 2·HK(μ₀, μ₁)² = 2L². Consistency check: nonnegativity of the resulting quadratic recovers the known 1-Lipschitz property |√m₁ − √m₀| ≤ HK(μ₀, μ₁).

Reduction theorem (v5.3). Triadic closure — any two distinctions determine a unique third, with the triad closed under the pair-to-third map — is exactly the Steiner triple system axiom set, and it does NOT entail the Fano plane: the affine plane AG(2,3) is a counterexample (nine points, twelve triads, full triadic closure, yet twelve disjoint triad pairs; verified in fano_reduction.py). Within triadic closure plus nondegeneracy (some distinction lies outside some triad), the following supplements are equivalent and each forces the Fano plane: (A) any two triads share an element; (B) every element lies in exactly three triads. Proof of (A): a point p off a line L = {a,b,c} lies on the three distinct lines pa, pb, pc, and any further line through p would have to meet L in one of a, b, c — so exactly three lines pass through p; in a Steiner triple system every point lies on (n−1)/2 lines, hence n = 7; the triple system on seven points is unique and is PG(2,2). Proof of (B): n = 2r + 1 = 7 directly. The quadrilateral axiom is then automatic.

6. Bidirectional Reading and Self-Reproduction
6.1 The Two Directions
The framework can be read in two directions. Read upward, it describes structure forming through recursive permutation — how complexity emerges from simpler foundations through phi-governed recursion. This reading produces hope: the building can continue.
Read downward, it describes structure dissolving through approach to the void — how the universe sinks toward zero with the structures we observe being patterns of the sinking rather than achievements of building. This reading produces dread: the bottom can’t be reached but the falling is real.
Both readings are accurate. Building gives infinite bounded complexity — structure that can never complete because each new level generates more relationships than can be tracked at the current level. Falling gives infinite unbounded nothingness — the void that can’t be reached because reaching it would mean being it. Neither direction completes. Both are simultaneously true.
The two directions as geometry (v5.1). With the corrected metric of Section 2.4, the bidirectional reading is no longer only interpretive. The Hellinger–Kantorovich geometry canonically decomposes every infinitesimal motion of μ into a transport component — rearrangement of shape at fixed mass, the Wasserstein channel — and a reaction component — creation and annihilation of mass, the Fisher–Rao channel pointing along the ray toward or away from the void. Building is motion in the transport channel; falling is motion in the reaction channel toward μ₀. The framework’s two readings are the two orthogonal channels of its own configuration-space metric. The void-resistance potential acts only on the reaction channel, which is why structure can transform freely while annihilation is resisted.
The configuration’s reality is neither building nor falling separately. It is the simultaneous condition of structure forming as it dissolves, with neither the formation nor the dissolution being prior. The 0-1-2-3 hierarchy is not a sequence pointing one direction. It is the configuration that exists as the simultaneous condition of structure forming and dissolving, with the 1 being where formation and dissolution meet, the 2 being how that meeting differentiates, the 3 being how the differentiation maintains itself temporarily, and the 0 being what the whole configuration exists against without ever resolving into.
6.2 Self-Reproduction
Local exhaustion produces stable form, but the universe keeps going. New stars form as old ones die. New cells form as old ones break down. The framework is self-reproducing at the structural level: each completed local instance generates conditions for new local instances because the structure that produced it is the same structure that produces them.
The pattern doesn’t end at any local exhaustion because the pattern itself is what makes new local instances possible. This is not infinity in the unbounded sense, but unending in the continuously-regenerating sense. The framework doesn’t close because closing would mean ending, and the structure’s nature is to not end.
6.3 The Calculator Bound
The framework opens and closes simultaneously because its limits are the calculator’s limits, not the framework’s limits. As far as any specific instance can compute the framework, it works. The point at which it stops working is the point at which the computation runs out of capacity, not the point at which the framework runs out of content. The framework is always closed at the boundary of what’s been computed and always open beyond that boundary.
Most successful theories are closed-and-open in this same way — complete in their applicable domain, incomplete at boundaries where calculation breaks down. General relativity is closed within tested regimes, opens at black hole singularities and the early universe. Quantum mechanics is closed within tested regimes, opens at the measurement problem and quantum gravity. The framework’s distinction is articulating this structure explicitly rather than treating boundary openness as embarrassing failure.
6.4 The Search as Instance
If the framework is correct, the search for an answer is itself an instance of what the framework describes. Looking up finds an answer because looking up is one of the ways the framework manifests. Looking down finds an answer because looking down is another. The search produces what it’s looking for because the search is the same recursive permutation that produces everything else.
This is not solipsism. It is what self-similar structure means when the seeker is part of the structure being sought. The framework couldn’t close from outside because the work of articulating it is already the framework operating. Every refinement is the structure refining its own articulation through one of its local instances.
