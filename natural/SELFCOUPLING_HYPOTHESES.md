# Gravity is subject to gravity — self-coupling of the joint theory's spin-2 field (hypotheses)

*Committed before `SELFCOUPLING_SCOPE.md`. Scoping only: nothing is built or adopted.*

## The principle under test

**The principle.** The spin-2 field of the joint theory (target 3, repaired) couples to all energy,
**including its own**.

**Where this sits in the joint scope.** JOINT_SCOPE (b) counted "field energy gravitates" once, as
the *all-energy* selection. Applied to gravity's own energy it is the same statement, so the
principle itself is **not a new selection** (SC4).

**The route: the self-coupling derivations.**
- Gupta 1954, Kraichnan 1955, Feynman's *Lectures on Gravitation* (1962–63), Deser 1970 ("Self-
  interaction and gauge invariance", GRG 1, 9), and Wald 1986 (PRD 33, 3613).
- The argument: the linear spin-2 field coupled to T_matter is inconsistent. Its field equation
  needs ∂_μT^μν = 0, but matter then exchanges energy-momentum with the field.
- Adding the field's own stress tensor as a source, and iterating, closes — in Deser's first-order
  form, in one step — on the Einstein–Hilbert action.

## Hypotheses

**SC1 — uniqueness: YES, with stated conditions.** The completion is fixed uniquely as general
relativity **plus a cosmological constant Λ**, under three conditions:
- **(a) Lorentz covariance of the linear theory.** Already selected by the spin-2 repair, but true
  on the lattice only in the continuum limit;
- **(b) two derivatives** — the lowest order. On the lattice that is nearest-neighbour minimality,
  a `main` principle, so it is not newly counted; higher-derivative terms (R², …) are allowed but
  suppressed by further scales;
- **(c) the field-theoretic stress-tensor ambiguities** (improvement terms, field redefinitions)
  are resolved as in Deser, or they amount to field redefinitions (Wald's general-covariance
  theorem).

**The known critiques** (Padmanabhan 2008, IJMPD 17, 367; Butcher, Hobson & Lasenby 2009) say the
derivation needs these assumptions; they do not produce a different two-derivative completion. **Λ
is not fixed:** it is +1 parameter (or a selection, Λ = 0).

**SC2 — the nonlinear lapse is fixed.**
- **Candidate 7's open choice is closed.** The static, spherically symmetric vacuum is
  Schwarzschild (Birkhoff). In isotropic coordinates the lapse is
  N = (1 − GM/2rc²)/(1 + GM/2rc²); in areal coordinates, √(1 − 2GM/rc²).
- **It is neither of candidate 7's two options.** Unlike the linear 1 + Φ, it does not produce
  negative energies. Unlike the exponential e^Φ, it does reach zero, at r = 2GM/c².
- **At first post-Newtonian order:** g₀₀ = −(1 − 2U + 2βU²) with **β = 1**, the U² term being
  gravity's gravitation. γ = 1 was already fixed by the spin-2 selection.
- **Note: the exponential lapse also gives β = 1** at this order. So β alone does not choose
  between them. The strong field does.

**SC3 — horizons become a consequence, not a choice. Predicted: YES, conditionally.**
- Within GR, any mass compressed inside 2GM/c² has a horizon (Birkhoff, Oppenheimer–Snyder), and
  collapse is inevitable above the maximum mass of any causal equation of state (Rhoades–Ruffini),
  given the energy conditions (Penrose).
- **A′'s matter satisfies the energy conditions**: its potential √5r²/2 + r³/3 is positive. So
  self-gravitating A′ lumps above their maximum mass must form horizons.
- **The linear-lapse "energy unbounded below"** of candidate 7 is replaced by the positive-energy
  theorem (Schoen–Yau, Witten).
- **The condition: this holds in the continuum.** GR's dynamical geometry conflicts with `main`'s
  **fixed lattice** (SC5).

**SC4 — the count.**
- **No selection is added.** Self-coupling is the all-energy selection already counted.
- **It pre-empts one:** the nonlinear-lapse form that candidate 7 would otherwise have needed. That
  one was never counted, so the listed total stays **7**.
- **Parameters: +1 (Λ).**
- **New firm predictions**, not used to choose the principle:
  - **(i) β = 1** — Mercury's perihelion advance, 42.98″/century, needs (2 + 2γ − β)/3 = 1 with
    γ = 1; the Nordtvedt effect is η = 4β − γ − 3 = 0 (LLR). Counted **once**;
  - **(ii) horizons and their dynamics** — black holes, Kerr ringdown (EHT, LIGO/Virgo). Counted
    **once**.
- **Firm predictions: 7 → 9.**
- **Not counted:**
  - light bending and tensor waves (they motivated spin-2);
  - Λ's value (fitted).
- **Conditions: ~9 → ~11.**

**SC5 — the conflict with `main`.**
- **GR's diffeomorphism invariance is incompatible with `main`'s fixed lattice as an exact
  symmetry.** The lattice is a background, and a preferred frame.
- **So the self-coupled theory is GR only in the continuum limit.** At the lattice scale ℓ:
  - diffeomorphism invariance is broken, and the extra modes of broken gauge invariance can
    appear (massive-gravity-type modes, a Boulware–Deser-like ghost);
  - the matter couplings must become metric-dependent (L1/L2-type couplings: the lapse on-site, the
    metric on bonds);
  - singularities are cut off at ℓ.
- **Counted as a tension, not a new selection** — the same premise (a lattice base) that makes the
  photon dispersion a prediction (candidate 5).

**SC6 — no change to G, so no help with the hierarchy.**
- Self-coupling adds only terms of higher order in GM/rc² (and Λ).
- The weak-field strength G — the coefficient of the linear coupling — is untouched.
- **It does not address λ ~ 10⁴⁰, the hierarchy KK_SCOPE and DILUTION_SCOPE left open.**

**Predicted verdict:**
- self-coupling fixes the completion as GR + Λ, under conditions (a)–(c);
- candidate 7's lapse is fixed, and horizons follow;
- no selection added (one pending selection pre-empted), +1 parameter (Λ), +2 firm predictions;
- in tension with the fixed lattice at scale ℓ;
- G, and so the hierarchy, untouched.
