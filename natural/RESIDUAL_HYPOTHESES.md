# Target 4 — the residual as the missing part: the frozen property list

*Committed before anything is derived from it. The later documents (`RESIDUAL_SCOPE.md`) may check,
cost and draw consequences from this list, but they may not edit it. A property found to be missing
later is recorded there as a **late addition**, counted, and flagged.*

## The hypothesis, in the Le Verrier / Pauli style

- **The precedents.** Le Verrier inferred Neptune from Uranus's residuals, and Pauli inferred the
  neutrino from the missing energy in beta decay. In each case the properties of the unseen thing
  were fixed by what was missing, and then tested by consequences nobody had asked for.
- **The hypothesis here.** Gravity is missing from `main`, and both CP-G pilots show why:
  - the empty vacuum is gapped;
  - the populated background has gapless densities but no long-range static response;
  - its one long-range effect, a sink's depletion, follows |u| rather than energy, and an A′
    packet feels it only as a scalar refraction ∝ 1/r³.
  So: **there is a residual** — a populated background of the P0 kind — **whose properties are
  exactly those needed for gravity to emerge as a shadow (Le Sage) force.**
- **The test is the unasked-for consequences**, as with Neptune and the neutrino.
