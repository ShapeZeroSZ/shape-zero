higher-dimensional gravity on the node's phase circle:
- the phase U(1) generated by 𝕁 is the KK gauge symmetry;
- κ is the potential, g_{0θ};
- the node's radial coordinate (MODEL_SPEC §0, the cone) is the KK scalar.

**Inputs.**

  (rotating-frame) energy.
- **The geometry.** The target metric of a node's internal ℂⁿ, written along the U(1) orbit, is
  dr² + r²(dθ + A)² + (the orbit-transverse part). r is the orbit's radius through the node's
  state; under A′ that is exactly the node's total radius |u|.

## K2 — the node's radial coordinate is not a KK scalar (predicted: the identification FAILS)

**What a KK scalar is:** the fibre's size at a point — one modulus shared by all matter there,
independent of what the matter does.

**What r is:** the matter's own position in the fibre — the orbit radius of its state. The charge
is L = r²θ̇ plus the κ term, and both it and r are matter variables. So **r is not an independent
modulus.**

**Partial resemblance:**
- under A′ one radius is shared by all n components at a site;
- `main`'s universal stiffness shift dK = |u| − |u_l| changes every component's frequency by
  dK/(2ω_a + κ), the same for both branches (MODEL_SPEC 4b.1; `main`'s pairs-and-refraction test,
  pending).

That mimics a radion's universal effect on KK masses, but with the wrong form:

|  | stiffness term | how it depends on the size | what it modulates |
|---|---|---|---|
| A′ (lattice) | additive: √5 + r | stiffness **rises** as the radius grows | not e |
| KK radion | multiplicative: p_θ²/R² | mass **falls** as the radius grows | e as well (1/e² ∝ R²) |

**Stabilisation:**
- the node well stabilises r — **at r = 0**, the cone's apex;
- **the KK circle has zero radius in A′'s vacuum**, where the reading is degenerate: e ∝ 1/R → ∞.

**KC2 (numerical):**
- the radial (amplitude) and angular (phase) fluctuations about the vacuum are not separate modes;
- the linearised spectrum at r = 0 contains only the two chirality branches, gapped at
  ω_a(0) = 1.0864 and ω_b(0) = 2.0582;
- there is no third, radion-like mode, at n = 1 (K = √5, κ\*).

**The only vacuum with R ≠ 0** is the ring (form B, |ψ| = φ-type minimum; `RING_FORK.md`). With a
dynamical u(1) there, the phase Goldstone is eaten. That is the Higgs phase: the **photon becomes
massive**, so there is no long-range electromagnetism. **So neither node form gives a KK vacuum
with an unbroken U(1).**

## K4 — the known failure modes appear (predicted: YES, both)

**(a) Charge-to-mass.**
- The KK modes satisfy the extremal relation λ ≡ e²q²/(4πG m²) = 1 + a² = **4** (a = √3, the
  D = 5 dilaton coupling). The radion's attraction (a² = 3) balances the electric repulsion
  (no-force).
- A 5D mass or the node well adds rest mass, so **the reading forces λ ≤ 4** for every charged
  state.
- **Nature's charged constituents have λ ~ 10⁴⁰–10⁴²** (the joint scope's λ; electron ≈ 4×10⁴²).
- **Predicted: the reading FAILS the charge-to-mass test by ~40 orders**, as classical KK does.
- **On the lattice: every quantum has the same gauge-invariant q/m.**
  - The gauge-invariant (rotating-frame) mass is ω_rot = ω_a + κ/2 = ω_b − κ/2 = √(√5 + κ²/4) =
    **1.5722** for both branches.
  - So |q|/m = **0.6361** = 2/(2ω_a + κ) (KC3, to 10⁻¹²).
  - A single universal ratio, as for a KK tower, cannot give nature's spread of q/m.

**(b) The extra scalar.**
- **In genuine KK:** a massless radion gives a Brans–Dicke scalar. For d = 1 extra dimension,
  ω = −(d − 1)/d = 0, so **γ = 1/2**, against Cassini's γ = 1 ± 2×10⁻⁵. Hence stabilisation must
  be added.
- **Here (K2):** there is no separate radion, since r is the matter's gapped amplitude (≥ 1.0864).
  **So the extra-scalar failure does not appear — but only because the reading is not a genuine
  KK reduction.**

**(c) Chirality.**
- Compactification on S¹ gives vector-like spectra.
- The a/b branches are the ±charge (±KK-momentum) partners, with equal gauge-invariant mass (1.5722).
- Their splitting κ is the uniform potential's ±eA₀.
- **Predicted: the "chirality branches" are charge-conjugate partners, not 4D chirality.**
  Consistent with EM_SCOPE §4.
