# Dilution of gravity over the populated tower — hypotheses and predictions

*Committed before `DILUTION_SCOPE.md` or any check. Scoping only: nothing is built.*

## The hypothesis under test

**KK_SCOPE.md's problem.** Charged states satisfy λ = e²/(4πGm²) ≤ 4, where nature has
λ ~ 10³⁶–10⁴² (proton to electron).

**The proposed solution, as in:**
- **ADD** (Arkani-Hamed, Dimopoulos, Dvali 1998): gravity spread over a large volume;
- **RS** (Randall, Sundrum 1999): gravity localised away by a warp factor.

**Here:**
- gravity couples to every level of `main`'s populated tower (P0: all levels at once);
- the electromagnetic U(1) lives only on the D ≤ 8 phase circle (components 0–3);
- so gravity's strength on D ≤ 8 matter is diluted by the tower's depth, while e is not.

**Inputs:**
- **A′'s coupling:** it is U(n)-symmetric. The only coupling between the tower and the D ≤ 8 part
  is the shared node radius |u|: `main`'s universal stiffness shift dK = |u| − |u_l|, and parametric
  heating (§6u). It is on-site (contact) and scalar.
- **The pilots:** every mode of `main` is gapped (pilot 1), and the populated background carries
  no long-range static response (pilot 2).
- **Gravity is an ingredient:** the spin-2 form of target 3 (the gravity-arc summary).

## D1 — is there a coupling that spreads gravity over the tower while charge stays on D ≤ 8?

**(a) Charge on D ≤ 8 only — ALLOWED, as a selection.**
- Gauge the U(1) generated by 𝕁 restricted to components 0–3, instead of 𝕁 on all components.
- It commutes with 𝕁, it is passive, and every component's U(1) charge is separately conserved
  (pilot 2, K4). So charge stays on D ≤ 8.
- **Cost:** it explicitly breaks A′'s U(n) level symmetry in the gauge and kinetic terms, down to
  U(4) × U(n − 4). That is 1 selection.
- **Side prediction:** gauging 𝕁 on *all* components would make the populated tower a charged
  plasma, whose Debye screening makes electromagnetism short-range. So the restriction is also
  needed for long-range EM at all.

**(b) Spreading gravity through `main`'s own coupling — IMPOSSIBLE.**
- The only tower–D ≤ 8 channel is A′'s shared radius. It is contact, scalar and gapped: it
  carries no propagating mediator, still less a spin-2 one.
- **And it scales the wrong way.** The shared radius is √(Σ_levels |u_i|²), so the tower's effect
  on D ≤ 8 matter *grows* with depth: dK ≈ ⟨|u_tower|⟩ ∝ √N·A_U at fixed per-level amplitude A_U.
  That is anti-dilution.
- **Predicted verdict: the scalar-radius coupling makes dilution impossible within `main`'s
  couplings.**

**(c) Dilution needs two added ingredients.** Principles-compatible, but not supplied:
- **(i) level-resolved gravity:** a gravitational field (lapse, or spin-2 h) per level, each
  coupled to that level's energy. It is passive and J-compatible (energy is J-invariant), but it
  breaks U(n);
- **(ii) an inter-level mixing that leaves one massless combination spread over all levels:** a
  "Laplacian on the tower".
- **A′'s level democracy (U(n)) fixes the mixing's form to the complete graph** (every level
  equivalent). So the N − 1 orthogonal modes are **degenerate**, at a single mass m, unlike ADD's
  tower of spacing 1/R.
- **Cost:** 2 selections, plus the parameters N (depth), m, and G_* (replacing G).

## D2 — scaling with depth

**Complete-graph (ADD-like) dilution.**
- D ≤ 8 matter couples to its own level's field with G_*. The massless mode is (1/√N)Σh_i, so at
  distances ≫ 1/m, **G_eff = G_*/N**, where N is the number of tower levels sharing the graviton.
- Equivalently M_Pl² = N·M_*², as in ADD's M_Pl² = M_*^{2+n}·V_n.

**The depth required:**

| requirement | N | Cayley–Dickson doublings d (N = 4·2^d complex components, D = 8·2^d) |
|---|---|---|
| **λ ratio ≈ 10⁴⁰** (user's figure; λ_* ~ 1) | 10⁴⁰ | **d ≈ 132** (D ≈ 2^135 ~ 10⁴¹) |
| M_* = 10 TeV (LHC-safe) | (M_Pl/M_*)² = 1.5×10³⁰ | **d ≈ 98** |
| M_* = 1 TeV | 1.5×10³² | d ≈ 105 |

**Warped (RS-like) alternative:**
- A chain ordering of the levels — the Cayley–Dickson doublings D8 ⊂ D16 ⊂ … are a natural chain —
  with a warp factor w < 1 per doubling gives G_eff/G_* ~ w^{2d}.
- The ratio 10⁴⁰ then needs **d ≈ 46/ln(1/w)** doublings; for example d ≈ 46 at w = e⁻¹.
- **A′'s U(n) makes every level equal, so w = 1 (no warp).** A warp breaks A′'s level democracy:
  1 selection, plus w as a parameter.
