*Scoping only; nothing is built and nothing is adopted. Hypotheses committed first:
`EM_HYPOTHESES.md` (7a166b8). Checks: `em_scope_checks.py` → `em_scope_checks_output.txt`

## Summary

A dynamical u(1) can be added **consistently**. The model's principles shape it more than
expected:
- **Passivity forces the covariant form** of the velocity links.
- **J-compatibility becomes a consistency requirement** of Gauss's law.

The long-wavelength limit at q = 3 gives Maxwell's equations, a two-polarisation photon and an
inverse-square static force. But those facts are what select the adaptation, so they add
nothing to the evidence.

**The adaptation adds two parameters and two discrete selections, and about one or two genuine
conditions.** The count gets worse: the strict predictions would equal the parameters, which is
fitting level.

**A blocker for "force between charges":** the adopted node well admits **no stable charged
lumps** (C5). The model's charges are travelling, dispersing wave packets, not static particles.

## 1. What makes the links dynamical, and what the principles say

**The ingredient.** A compact u(1) field θ_{n,μ} on every nearest-neighbour bond, with three
pieces:
- **Transport.** It dresses **every** nearest-neighbour coupling of the charged (𝕁) sector with
  U = exp(𝕁θ): the elastic hop as a Peierls rotation (C2: conservative to 7×10⁻¹⁰ — finite
  difference — and covariant to 9×10⁻¹⁶), and the existing velocity links as M = W·U.
- **Electric energy** ½M Σ θ̇².
- **Magnetic energy**, a Wilson plaquette term Λ Σ_P (1 − cos θ_P).

In temporal gauge, local covariance of every term makes **Gauss's law a conserved constraint**
of the dynamics (standard; Kogut–Susskind). At θ ≡ 0 every coupling reduces exactly to
`main`'s. But a charged wave sources an electric field, so `main` is the **e → 0 limit** of the
extended model, not a sector of it.

**Passivity (H1 — FAILED as stated; the corrected statement is stronger).**
- The hypothesis said passivity forbids the phase on the velocity links. That is true only for
  the model's **literal** form c(W_n v_{n+1} − W_{n−1} v_{n−1}), where W = aI + b𝕁 does work
  (C1: power 0.73).
- The **covariant** form transports the reverse hop with the transpose,
  c(M_n v_{n+1} − M_{n−1}ᵀ v_{n−1}). Its coupling matrix is antisymmetric, so it does no work
  for **any** M. That covers the dressed u(2) links M = W·U(θ) (C7: power 2×10⁻¹⁵, covariance
  3×10⁻¹⁵); the literal form dressed the same way does work (power 16) and is not covariant.
- For symmetric W the two forms coincide, which is why mission 2 did not need to tell them
  apart.
- **So passivity plus covariance force the transposed form:** the model's existing links extend
  to the gauged theory unchanged at θ = 0. Passivity says nothing about the plaquette form.

**J-compatibility (H2 — HELD, with more).**
- It selects the link group: U(1) generated by 𝕁 itself, which commutes with every
  J-compatible coupling.
- It also becomes **required, not chosen**. Gauss's law ∇·E = eρ needs the source current to be
  locally conserved. A J-breaking coupling does not conserve phase charge, so it would violate
  Gauss's law. On `main`, J-compatibility is a principle with a derived floor κ ≥ κ\*; with a
  dynamical u(1) it is a consistency condition.

**Not selected by either principle:** Wilson vs Villain vs non-compact plaquette energy. That is
a discrete selection (H2 held). The classical theory differs between them only through
topological defects (monopoles), rare at weak coupling.

## 2. Parameters added (H3 — HELD)

Three new constants: M, Λ and the charge scale e (U = exp(𝕁eθ)). One is removable by rescaling
θ, leaving **two dimensionless parameters**:
- **the photon speed** c_γ = √(Λ/M), relative to the matter wave speed √c;
- **the coupling strength.**

A single light cone (c_γ = √c) is **selected by a physical fact** (Lorentz invariance), so it
is counted and cannot be predicted. **No relation fixes e from ĉ, κ̂ or β̂** (§4).

**Discrete selections**, counted under the charter:
- the compact Wilson form;
- **the q = 3 base.** The model runs at q = 1, 2 and 3, and only q = 3 gives an inverse-square
  force (§3), so choosing it is selection by that fact.

**Net: +2 parameters (one selected, one free) and +2 discrete selections.**

## 3. The long-wavelength limit (H4 — HELD, one reason corrected)

- **Photon** (C3, the linearised Wilson energy is the lattice curl-curl operator). At q = 3 there
  are **two** polarisations with ω² = c_γ²·Σ 4 sin²(k_i/2) exactly, and one zero mode — the
  longitudinal one, removed by Gauss's law. q = 2 has one polarisation; q = 1 has none. At long
  wavelength the dispersion is ω = c_γ|k|. Maxwell's equations follow with θ → aA (standard).
- **Static force of a point charge** (C4). The ratio to the continuum law, after removing the
  periodic box's neutralising background exactly, is:

  | q | law | r = 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3 | inverse-square, 1/(4πr²) | 1.066 | 1.015 | 1.003 | 0.988 |
  | 2 | 1/(2πr) — a log potential | 1.015 | 1.004 | 1.000 | 0.987 |
  | 1 | constant — a linear potential | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

  The r = 4 departures are lattice corrections; the r = 32 ones are periodic images at L/4.
  **The inverse-square law is realised only at q = 3.**
- **Corrected reason:** H4 cited Polyakov confinement at q = 2. That is a quantum (or thermal)
  monopole effect. The classical q = 2 theory is not confining, but its force is 1/r, not
  inverse-square. The conclusion stands; the reason was wrong.
- **Charges (new finding, C5).** A static force "between charges" needs static charged objects.
  The adopted node form A′ has V = √5 r²/2 + r³/3, and a Q-ball exists only if 2V/r² < K for some
  r (Coleman). Here 2V/r² = K + 2r/3 > K for every r > 0, so **the model has no stable charged
  lumps.**
  - Its charges are travelling wave packets, which disperse; the inverse-square law applies to
    external or imposed sources.
  - Supplying static charges would need a Q-ball-supporting self-interaction. That is a change to
    the node form `main` adopted, and it would be a further adaptation.

## 4. The charge, β̂, and the links' existing role (H5 — HELD)

- **The charge is the 𝕁-phase Noether charge**, the "total phase charge" that J-compatibility
  conserves (MODEL_SPEC §3). For a wave e^{∓𝕁ωt} its density is ±ω|ψ|². The two rotation senses —
  the a and b branches that κ splits — are the two signs of charge.
- **β̂ is not the charge's coupling.**
  - β sits on the **scalar** sector: a real, uncharged field that no u(1) couples to.
  - Its lattice action is a Peierls angle arctan(βω) that grows with frequency (C6: 0.10, 0.16
    and 0.30 at carrier frequencies 2.06, 3.31 and 6.15), whereas a u(1) phase shifts every mode
    by the same θ.
  - In the continuum β is a uniform drift (MODEL_SPEC §1c): it couples to momentum density, like
    a shift vector g₀ᵢ. **β belongs to target 2**, not target 1.
- **The existing 𝕁-sector links** (the u(n) segments of gates 7 and 8) stay static background
  fields, dressed covariantly (§1). At long wavelength their trace part is a frame and their
  traceless part is a static su(n) background (MODEL_SPEC §1c, "links"). Neither is the photon.
- **κ and A₀.** The lab-frame Lagrangian ½|(∂_t − μ𝕁)ψ|² with μ = κ/2 (MODEL_SPEC §1c) is exactly
  ½|D_tψ|² with a uniform potential eA₀ = μ. So **Larmor's theorem is a gauge transformation**,
  and the Larmor splitting κ is the ±e charges' energy difference 2eA₀.
  - With A₀ dynamical, a uniform κ is removable except through the mass shift κ²/4. That agrees
    with `main`'s "κ̂ enters only through ĉ′".
  - **OPEN, not checked:** H5's further claim that a spatially varying κ acts as an electric field
    −∇κ/(2e).

## 5. What would exceed the inputs — the count (H6 — HELD in expectation)

**Used to select the adaptation, so worth nothing** (charter rule 3): Maxwell's equations, the
photon, its two polarisations, the inverse-square force, the light cone c_γ = √c, and Gauss's
law.

**Candidate genuine conditions:**
1. **The lattice photon dispersion shape** Σ 4 sin²(k_i/2) beyond the one point that fixes c_γ —
   the analogue of `main`'s sin k condition. It is testable only in an engineered realisation;
   natural photons show no lattice departure.
2. **κ gradient ≡ electric field** — OPEN; it counts only if verified and measurable
   independently of e.
3. **A relation fixing e** — none found. e stays free.
