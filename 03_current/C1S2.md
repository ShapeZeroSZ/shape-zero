## 1. The base is forced

C1S left the base unconstrained: persistence was shown to force the *fibre*
definite, and nothing in that argument touches base signature. Applying the same
principle where evolution happens closes it.

**Signature.** Persistence retains only bounded, enduring motion. Motion not
determined by its initial data does not endure — it is not motion. So
well-posedness of the Cauchy problem is a persistence requirement, not an added
assumption, and well-posedness is a signature question with a known answer.

| base signature | character | Cauchy problem |
|---|---|---|
| (0,q) | elliptic | **ill posed** (Hadamard) |
| (1,q) | hyperbolic | **well posed** |
| (p,q), p ≥ 2 | ultrahyperbolic | **ill posed** |

Measured on data φ = 0, ∂_tφ = 10⁻³ sin(kx) evolved to t = 1:

- hyperbolic response **falls as 1/k**: 8.4×10⁻⁴ → 1.9×10⁻⁴ → 1.8×10⁻⁵
- elliptic response **grows as e^{kt}/k**: 1.2×10⁻³ → 6.8×10⁻³ → **2.8×10²**

At k = 16 an elliptic perturbation of size 10⁻³ produces a response of 278 —
amplification of 3×10⁵ in one time unit, unbounded in k. No constant relates
output to data. Ultrahyperbolic fails identically: data on one time-slice leaves
the second time evolution unconstrained.

**So persistence forces exactly one timelike direction.** The same principle
gives opposite signatures on the two sides — the fibre is where states live and
must be bounded, the base is where evolution happens and must be well posed.

**Dimension.** Two principles squeeze from opposite sides.

| q | persistence: stable bound orbits | plurality: graviton dof D(D−3)/2 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | — | **0**, topological, no local dynamics |
| **3** | **stable orbit** | **2**, waves exist |
| 4 | no minimum (both terms ~1/r²) | 5 |
| 5+ | attraction dominates, collapse | 9 |

Persistence caps q ≤ 3 (Ehrenfest's argument, 1917 — the constraint is not new;
what is new is that it comes from a principle already in the ladder). Plurality
bounds q ≥ 3. **q = 3 uniquely, base = (1,3).**

*Caveat:* the q = 2 exclusion rests on the graviton count, not on the orbit
numerics, which mishandled the logarithmic potential. The plurality bound is the
clean one there.

**Verification that nothing forbids this base:** a Euclidean fibre over a
Lorentzian base is stable — max|φ| = 1.507, unchanged from free evolution, with
energy 0.392 → 0.383. An indefinite fibre over the same base runs away: max|φ| =
599, energy climbing 58×. **The obstruction is entirely in the fibre.** Gravity
is absent from the ladder, not excluded by it.

## 2. The field equations are forced

Lovelock's theorem: in D = 4 the only divergence-free symmetric rank-2 tensor
built from the metric with at most second derivatives is aG_{μν} + bg_{μν}.
**Every hypothesis is a principle already owned:**

| hypothesis | source |
|---|---|
| dimension 4 | §1 above |
| divergence-free | **conservativity** |
| at most 2nd derivatives | **persistence** (Ostrogradsky: H linear in a momentum, unbounded below) |

Lovelock term count = ⌊(D−1)/2⌋: **D = 3 → 0** (Einstein tensor identically
zero), **D = 4 → 1** (Einstein), **D = 5 → 2** (Gauss–Bonnet becomes dynamical,
uniqueness lost). **D = 4 is the unique dimension where gravity's field
equations are both non-trivial and unique.**

That is why B-3 did not connect before: D = 4 had to be assumed.

*Not derived:* the couplings. Lovelock fixes the form aG + bg, not a or b, so G
and Λ remain free — consistent with the dimensionless-inputs argument, since both
are dimensionful.

*Failed demonstration, recorded:* an attempt to show Ostrogradsky runaway
numerically with a Pais–Uhlenbeck oscillator stayed bounded (2.72, 2.37). The
instability is real and follows from H being linear in p₁, but the numerics
contributed nothing and the coupling used did not drive the resonance.

## 3. Electromagnetism, and its relation to gravity

The same derivation pattern applied to a connection instead of a metric.

Gauge invariance plus Lorentz invariance at second order admits exactly **two**
scalars quadratic in F — rank 2, verified. F_{μν}F̃^{μν} integrates to
**0.000e+00** on a periodic domain: topological, a total derivative, no field
equations. **One non-trivial term survives: Maxwell.**

| | object | candidates | survivor |
|---|---|---|---|
| gravity | metric on the **base** | Λ, R | G_{μν} + Λg_{μν} |
| EM | connection on the **fibre** | F², FF̃ | Maxwell |

**Their relation is the base–fibre split itself.** Kaluza–Klein counting closes
exactly: a 5D metric has **15** components = **10** (4D metric) + **4** (vector)
+ **1** (scalar). Gravity on base × fibre *is* gravity plus electromagnetism plus
a scalar.

**The radion does not arise here.** In standard KK the fibre is a circle of
arbitrary radius, so the modulus is flat and the radion massless. In this
construction the fibre scale is rigid: deforming the imaginary products by λ
destroys composition immediately — error 1.4×10⁻¹ at λ = 1.01 against 3.6×10⁻¹⁵
at λ = 1 — and the admissible structures are 16 discrete points with no
continuous family. **𝕆 is rigid; rigidity is the absence of moduli; the radion
is a modulus.**

*Caveat:* rigidity shown is of the algebra. The radion is a field on the base,
and converting this into a statement about the effective 4D theory requires the
compactification done properly. Note also that rigidity removes the freedom
everywhere, not only where it was a problem.

## 4. Gauge structure: contact, not product

An early claim that D4's u(2) and D8's su(3) *combine* into the Standard Model
algebra is **retracted**. The centraliser of su(3) in g₂ is **zero-dimensional**
— nothing commutes with it — and su(3) is maximal in g₂, so su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1)
does **not** embed. Dimensions from different rungs were added without checking
simultaneous realisability.

**The correct question is a coset one.** Requiring both structures at once —
fixing a point (the D8 condition) *and* a quaternion subalgebra (the D4
condition) — leaves a subalgebra of **dimension 4**, bracket-closed to
2.2×10⁻¹⁵, with a **1-dimensional centre**: su(2) ⊕ u(1) = **u(2)**. The same
u(2) passivity forces at D4, recovered as what survives when both rungs are in
contact.

And the split is canonical:

| | dim | role |
|---|---|---|
| u(2) | 4 | acts **within** one quaternion subalgebra — isospin |
| m | 4 | **moves between** subalgebras — ℂP² |
| su(3) | 8 | both — colour |

m is **not** a subalgebra and [m,m] ⊆ u(2) — a **symmetric space**, residuals
3.7×10⁻¹⁶ and 1.1×10⁻¹⁵, with the orbit of the subalgebra measured at dimension
4 = dim ℂP². **Colour and isospin are transverse halves of one object**, not
independent factors needing a common container.

**Representation content that follows.** Im(𝕆) under su(3) has Casimir spectrum
0 (×1) and 4/3 (×6) — exactly two irreducibles, so **Im(𝕆) = 1 ⊕ 3 ⊕ 3̄**: one
colour singlet and one colour triplet, a lepton and a quark, from the algebra
rather than assumed. Under u(2), Im(𝕆) = **1 ⊕ 2 ⊕ 4** (Casimir 0, 1/3, 5/6):
what is inside ℍ is singlet-like, what is transverse is the doublet.

**Also forced, given the representations:** anomaly cancellation overdetermines
hypercharge — 5 unknowns, 3 independent linear conditions leaving a 2-plane, and
the cubic condition cutting that to 3 discrete rays, one of which is the Standard
Model assignment exactly (all four anomalies vanish, cubic at 2.2×10⁻¹⁶). And
sin²θ_W = Tr(T₃²)/Tr(Q²) = **3/8** in exact rational arithmetic, with the GUT
normalisation Tr(Y²)/Tr(T₃²) = **5/3** appearing on its own.

*Honest placement:* 3/8 is Georgi–Glashow 1974, and the octonion→su(3) results
are Günaydin–Gürsey 1973 and later Furey. Arriving here independently is a
consistency check, not a discovery. The measured sin²θ_W is 0.231 and the gap is
renormalisation-group running, which needs particle content and a scale
hierarchy — neither of which this frame has.

*Not obtained:* field identifications (nothing says the doublet is Q rather than
L), and generations.

geometry," and it holds.

## 4c. The two dials, and what each exclusion cost

Each principle earns its place by excluding something, and three exclusions are
now measured.

| principle | kept | discarded | what the discard contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| **conservativity** | u(2), dim 4 | Hermitian, dim 4 | **amplification and dissipation** |
| **persistence** | definite branch | split branch | null cones, indefinite signature, escape |
| **plurality** | ≥ 2 dof | 1 dof | at D8: 14 terms → **0** |
| minimality | few parameters | families | not obviously structured |

gl(2,ℂ) on ℝ⁴ is 8-dimensional and splits **4 + 4**: conservativity keeps the
anti-Hermitian half exactly. Norm ratio after evolution 1.010 versus 1.186 — the
discarded half is precisely gain and loss. **So the ladder is closed because two
dials are set to closed:** nothing may enter or leave at the coupling level, and
nothing may depart a bounded region at the orbit level.

Plurality is not an opposite at all — turning it off empties the rung rather than
opening a complement. It is a threshold, not a switch.

**The switch-to-reality map**, three of four confirmed:

| conservativity | persistence | regime |
|---|---|---|
| on | on | Hamiltonian mechanics, spectra, bound states |
| **off** | on | open systems, dissipation, lasers |
| on | **off (base)** | relativistic field theory |
| off | off | radiation damping, open relativistic systems |

The fourth fails: relaxing persistence on the **fibre** gives ghosts —
negative-norm states — which physics removes by constraint wherever they appear.
So the switches are not free-standing; **where they are applied decides whether
the flip opens a regime or a pathology**, and only the base tolerates the
persistence flip.

**The split branch is not degenerate — it is the other half of Hurwitz.**

**Derivation dimensions are identical on both branches** — same algebraic
skeleton, non-compact real forms (so(2,1) for so(3), g₂(2) for g₂). Split-complex
numbers *are* 2D Minkowski; Im(split-ℍ) is **3D Minkowski** with Aut = SO(2,1).

**But (1,3) appears nowhere on either branch.** Four-dimensional spacetime shows

**And the 112 discarded orientations are not broken.** They remain 8-dimensional

**Which is the honest characterisation of what the ladder is.** Composition is

more defensible than "derivation of reality."

## 4e. The coherence staircase

Generation is unbounded — Cayley–Dickson never stops, and each doubling switches
on a new failure. What produces the rungs is **which coherence is still demanded**.

| law demanded | last dimension it holds | span of commutators | span of associators |
|---|---|---|---|
| commutative | **2** | 0 at dim 1, 2 | 0 at dim 1, 2, 4 |
| associative | **4** | 3 at dim 4 | — |
| composition | **8** | 7 at dim 8 | 7 at dim 8 |
| power-associative | never fails | 15 at dim 16 | 15 at dim 16 |

ℝ and ℂ generate nothing: every product is already present, both spans zero. **ℍ
turns on the commutator; 𝕆 turns on the associator.** Each rung is the last
dimension satisfying one more law, and the tower continues past all of them with
only power-associativity and flexibility intact (verified to 10⁻¹⁴ at dimensions
16 and 32).

So Hurwitz reads as a **record of where each law gives out**, not as a fence.
Generativity alone selects "as large as possible"; coherence alone selects "as
small as possible"; **the balance point is wherever the coherence demand is set**,
and demanding composition puts it at 8. This reframes rather than derives — it does
not say why composition is the right demand rather than associativity, which would
put the top rung at ℍ.

## 5. D16: where homogeneity ends

**Der saturates at 14** — verified at dimensions 8, 16 and 32 — while the space
keeps doubling. Once 14 < dim of the unit sphere, the automorphism group cannot
act transitively.

| dim | dim Der | sphere | max orbit | homogeneous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 14 | S⁷ | 6 | no (codim 1, the real axis) |
| 16 | 14 | S¹⁵ | **11** | **no — codim 4** |

**Four continuous invariants**, all constant to 10⁻¹⁶ under random Der elements
against a control drifting at 7.8×10⁻²:

**Re(a)**, **Re(q)**, **|p|²**, **p·q**

Every one is inherited from the Cayley–Dickson doubling — two real parts, one
norm, one overlap. The non-homogeneity is *structured*: orbits are labelled by
how the two octonion halves sit relative to each other, and by nothing else.

**Zero divisors are confined to a single point in that invariant space.** Rank of
L_a drops from 16 to **12 — exactly, every time** — on precisely the locus
Re(p) = Re(q) = 0, |p| = |q|, p·q = 0, i.e. (0, 0, ½, 0). Generic sedenions are
invertible; imaginary-but-not-orthogonal are invertible. The kernel is always
**4-dimensional**, never 1, 2 or 3.

**That locus is a single homogeneous orbit.** Dimension count p ∈ S⁶ then q ∈ S⁵
gives **11**; measured orbit dimension **11**; stabiliser **3**, bracket-closed
at 1.4×10⁻¹⁵. The singular locus is **G₂/SU(2)**.

**So the wildness is the most ordered part.** Zero divisors — what breaks
composition and division — live on one homogeneous space with constant-rank
degeneracy. The *generic* sedenions are the non-homogeneous ones.

**The stabiliser identified.** A single generator has distinct |Im eigenvalue|
ratio **[1.0]** with 8 zeros, consistently across all three generators. No 3:1,
so no j = 3/2. Eight non-zero eigenvalues at one magnitude gives four j = ½
doublets, so the sedenion 16 branches as **8·(j=0) ⊕ 4·(j=½)**, and since
ℝ¹⁶ = 1 ⊕ 7 ⊕ 1 ⊕ 7 under g₂, each **7 → 3·(j=0) ⊕ 2·(j=½)**.

That is not the principal su(2) (which would give 7 as a single j = 3 with ratios
1:2:3). It is the su(2) inside the quaternion-subalgebra stabiliser — structurally
right, since the zero-divisor locus is defined by a quaternion-like condition.

**Method note:** two earlier attempts at this branching failed, both on
normalisation. The successful route drops normalisation entirely and uses
eigenvalue *ratios*, which are scale-free.

### 5b. The non-round metric, and the reduced eigenvalue problem

**A retraction first.** It was argued here that D16 cannot supply the missing
non-homogeneous geometry, on the grounds that S¹⁵ with the sedenion norm is the
*round* sphere — |a|² = Σaᵢ², SO(16) transitive, spectrum k(k+14) — so the
Der-invariant sector is only a sub-tower of a quadratic tower. **That argument is
wrong**, and its own inputs refute it: Δ_S u₁ = −15u₁ and Δ_S u₄ = −32u₄ are
exactly 1(1+14) and 2(2+14), which was visible two steps before the conclusion.

The error: the ambient Euclidean metric is not the algebra's metric once
composition fails.

| | σ_min range | σ_max range | \|det L_a\| range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D8** | 1.0000–1.0000 | 1.0000–1.0000 | 1.0000–1.0000 |
| **D16** | **0.102–0.853** | **1.128–1.411** | **0.0004–0.858** |

At D8, |xy| = |x||y| makes L_a an isometry — algebra metric and round metric
coincide, which is *why* every octonionic spectrum in this program came out as
known group theory. **At D16 composition fails and L_a distorts**, by a factor
varying eightfold across the sphere. **The failure of composition is itself the
source of a non-round geometry**, appearing at exactly the dimension where
homogeneity ends, for the same reason.

**The distortion is Der-invariant**, verified directly: L_{g(a)} = g L_a g⁻¹ to
2×10⁻¹⁶, so det and every singular value drift at 10⁻¹⁶ or exactly zero. It
therefore descends to the four-invariant quotient.

**The full reduction, every piece exact.** For an invariant function
f(u₁,u₂,u₃,u₄), Δf = Σ g^{ij}∂_i∂_j f + Σ (Δu_i)∂_i f, and both coefficient sets
close in the invariants.

*Metric* (max error 1.1×10⁻¹⁵ over 300 points):

|---|---|---|---|
| g¹¹ = 1 − u₁² | g¹² = −u₁u₂ | g¹³ = 2u₁(1−u₃) | g¹⁴ = u₂ − 2u₁u₄ |

**Δu = (−15u₁, −15u₂, 16−32u₃, −32u₄)**

*Conformal weight:* det(L_a) is a **degree-6 polynomial in the invariants with
33 nonzero integer coefficients** — 64, −64, 32, −40 and the like, all multiples

    u₃ − u₁² ≥ 0                                    (|p′|² ≥ 0)

**g is positive definite throughout the interior**: smallest eigenvalue never

**The zero-divisor point is the geometric centre in three independent senses.**

**Status:** a positive-definite second-order operator with polynomial

## 6. What the model can and cannot produce

**Structural cap, and it is a theorem.** All five inputs — existence,
conservativity, persistence, minimality, plurality — are dimensionless.
Conservativity says a quantity vanishes; persistence is a topological condition
on an orbit; minimality and plurality are counts. Rescale everything and all five
read identically, so no derived quantity can carry a unit. **Every output in the
entire program is a pure number**: 1/2/3 harmonic ratios, orbit constants 2 and
6, ‖c‖² = 42, 16 of 128, dim Der = 14, 7 → 28, 22/8/14, associator 0 or 4,
Casimirs 0, 1/3, 4/3, 5/6, sin²θ_W = 3/8, multiplicities 1, 3, 3̄.

**The escape route, and its result.** Dimensionless *ratios* are not forbidden,
and rigid geometry gives forced spectra: m_n²/m_m² = λ_n/λ_m, with the scale
cancelling and nothing tunable.

| space | m² ratios | degeneracies |
|---|---|---|
| S² | 1, 3, 6, 10 | 3, 5, 7, 9 |
| S⁶ | 1, 7/3, 4, 6 | 7, 27, 77, 182 |
| S⁷ | 1, 16/7, 27/7, 40/7 | 8, 35, 112, 294 |
| ℂP² | 1, 8/3, 5, 8 | **8, 27, 64, 125** = (k+1)³ |

**Tested against measurement, and it fails.** Charged-lepton m² ratios are 1,
4.3×10⁴, 1.2×10⁷ — off by four to seven orders, unfixable by rescaling since
ratios are scale-free. Hadron Regge trajectories grow **linearly** in n; every
spectrum here grows **quadratically** as k(k+n−1) — a mismatch of functional
form, not of number. S² reproducing 1, 3, 6, 10 matches molecular rotation
**vacuously**: a rigid rotor *is* the Laplacian on S². The ℂP² degeneracies are
the SU(3) (k,k) representations beginning at the adjoint — apt, but standard.

**So the bound is numerical rather than rhetorical.** Compact homogeneous spaces
have quadratic spectra; physics' unexplained numbers do not live in quadratic
towers. Reaching them requires a non-homogeneous geometry or a solved interacting
problem — which is precisely why §5 matters, and why its spectrum is the one
open item with a route to a genuinely new number.

**D1 remains the only rung that ever solved rather than classified**, and the
integer lattice is robust: on the actual nonlinear potential, harmonic ratios are
1, 2, 3, 4 at both ε = 0.05 and ε = 0.20, amplitudes falling 1 → 0.003 → 0.000.

## 8. Open, in order of tractability

1. **Solve the reduced D16 eigenvalue problem (§5b).** Fully specified: exact
   metric, exact drift, degree-6 integer conformal weight, explicit domain with
   analytic faces, positive-definite interior. A 4D solver, not a research
   problem. **The only operator in the construction whose spectrum is not fixed
   in advance by representation theory** — because it comes from the *algebra's*
   metric, which stops being round exactly where composition fails.
2. **Laplacian spectrum on G₂/SU(2)** — tractable, branching now in hand, a
   finite calculation. But **G₂/SU(2) is homogeneous**, so its spectrum is
   Casimir eigenvalues — group theory, another quadratic tower, the shape §6
   already showed fails. Worth computing as a check; it cannot deliver what §6
   says is missing. *(This inverts an earlier ordering in which it was ranked
   first — the tractable half of D16 is not the half that matters.)*
3. **B-2** — the π/2 horizon is the Fisher–Rao diameter and matches the
   transport–reaction crossover already named in the spec; candidate β = 1/4.
   Closing condition: shape space is a simplex with boundary, and the deflection
   law needs a closed angle.
4. **Generations** — no basis-independent 3 exists in 𝕆. The three Fano lines
   through a point are one su(3) orbit (finite elements connect them at ~10⁻¹²),
   so the discrete 3 is a basis artifact. Any generation structure requires a
   genuinely new object.
5. **β notation collision** — the symbol denotes both the cone deficit and the
   gyroscopic coupling ratio. Must be fixed before external circulation.
